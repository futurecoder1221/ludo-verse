📖 How to Play

  • Roll a 6 to bring a token onto the board.
  • Move your token according to the dice number.
  • Landing on an opponent's token captures it unless the square is safe.
  • Rolling a 6 gives you another turn.
  • Move all four tokens to the finish to win.
